Level 0 Engine · Procedural Raycasting & Creative Determinism

The dark is not empty.

Level 0 Engine is not quite a game, and not quite not a game. It is an exploration of mathematical recursiveness and creative determinism. It is also a procedural 3D raycaster running natively in the browser with zero build steps and no asset packs. Instead of downloading gigabytes of pre-baked textures, it computes its liminal yellowing wallpaper, damp carpets, and fluorescent drone entirely on the fly from a single seed. Settle the cold case, restore the grid, and file the finding of fact before the maze takes you.

FACILITY.DOC 02 / 05 - ARCHITECTURE [ □ ]

Architecture & Engine

Procedural math, native to the browser.

The engine runs on native ES6 modules and WebGL via Three.js with zero build steps and no heavy asset pipelines. An O(1) Spatial Hash Grid powers instantaneous collision queries, zero-allocation scratch vectors eliminate garbage-collection stutter, and a fixed hardware shadow pool with hysteresis keeps dynamic lighting smooth and atmospheric without popping.

SURVIVAL.TXT 03 / 05 - FIELD MANUAL [ □ ]

The rules of the labyrinth

How to survive

The facility generated a unique cold case just for you. Find notes, laptops, and tapes to unravel it. Unverified documents burden your mind. You must corroborate claims by finding matching evidence in entirely different sectors.

  1. 0x01

    Manage your sanity

    The Flashlight & Mental Load

    The dark exacts a toll. Carrying unverified claims and roaming through pitch-black sectors accumulates tension, degrading your flashlight's maximum charge. Corroborate claims across sectors to settle facts and restore your sanity baseline. If your psyche fractures below 50%, the walls begin to hallucinate.

    Sanity
  2. 0x02

    The wandering hunter

    The Anomaly

    It stalks by sight and sound, accelerating the more exhausted you become. Catching the Anomaly directly in your focused beam freezes it in its tracks, but drives up its agitation. If it overtakes you, reality collapses and re-seeds around an entirely new cold case.

    Entity
  3. 0x03

    Sector anomalies

    Environmental Hazards

    Each macro-sector carries its own localized rules. In the Incinerator, never break eye contact with the Ember. In the Archive, the Archivist observes from the periphery. Whatever you do, do NOT sprint toward it. In the Atrium, keep moving under overhead shelves; lingering in the open invites the ceiling rig down.

    Sectors
  4. 0x04

    Restore the grid

    Surge Breakers

    The exit elevator requires full auxiliary power. You must navigate the labyrinth to locate three rare Dimensional Breakers. Throwing each switch triggers a temporary sector blackout before the local grid stabilizes.

    Power
  5. 0x05

    Submit the truth

    Finding of Fact

    Once all breakers are thrown and the elevator key is retrieved, the exit dynamically manifests in the next unvisited sector. The terminal demands your Finding of Fact: submit the correct verdict based on corroborated evidence. File the truth to escape; file wrong, and the anomaly claims the record.

    Verdict
