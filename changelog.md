### Changed

- **[ENGINE] Every Direct Light-Intensity Assignment Compensated For The `useLegacyLights` Migration (`Sectors.js`, `AtmosphereManager.js`, `LumenGrid.js`, 6 entity files, `InteractionController.js`, `InquestController.js`):** The removal of `renderer.useLegacyLights`/`THREE.ColorManagement.enabled` earlier this session was a deliberate move off legacy lighting, not a regression to revert - but every light-intensity constant in the codebase (`ambientLight`'s `0.30`, the flashlight's `1.4`, every `LumenGrid` pooled fixture, every entity's dedicated light) was still authored under the old model. Three.js's `WebGLLights.setup` applies an implicit `x Math.PI` to every non-ambient light's color*intensity under legacy mode and drops it entirely otherwise, so every one of those lights was rendering roughly a third as bright as intended - read most visibly as the flashlight and room fill lights barely illuminating anything. Added `LEGACY_LIGHT_COMPENSATION = Math.PI` and applied it at the ~20 call sites that actually assign a `THREE.Light.intensity` (not to the underlying `baseIntensity` data feeding them, since several of those also drive a fixture's own `emissiveIntensity`, which was never affected by the flag and would have been wrongly over-brightened alongside it).
- **[ENGINE] Pooled Room-Light Falloff Softened From Physically-Correct Back Toward Legacy's Feel (`LumenGrid.js`):** The `x Math.PI` fix above corrected overall brightness, but physically-correct inverse-square falloff (`decay: 2.0`, three.js's non-legacy default) is a fundamentally different *curve* from legacy's - retaining roughly 70-85% of peak brightness across most of a light's radius (verified against both GLSL formulas directly), versus physically-correct's ~10-30% at the same distance. Even at matched overall brightness, ceiling fixtures read as intensely bright at the source with almost nothing reaching the floor a few units below. `LumenGrid`'s pooled point/spot lights moved from `decay: 2.0` to `1.0`, matching the convention the flashlight and several entity lights (`WardenEntity`, etc.) already used.
- **[ENGINE] Gamma/Exposure Default Consolidated Back To `0.70` (`RenderEngine.js`, `engine.html`, `SaveManager.js`):** The same session that removed `useLegacyLights` had bumped the exposure default `0.70 -> 1.40` as a brightness band-aid, but left `SaveManager.js`'s own fallback (applied to any save missing a `gamma` key, which runs *after* `RenderEngine` already constructed with the new default) still on the stale `0.70` - silently halving exposure again for exactly that case. With the light-intensity fix above addressing the actual cause of the dimness, `1.40` on top of it overshot; reverted all three call sites back to the original `0.70`, restoring consistency between them.
- **[WORLD] Archive's Hanging Bowl Light And Housing Retuned (`SetPieces.js`):** `buildHangingBowlLight`'s default `intensity` raised `1.5 -> 3.2`; its shade-derived `spotAngle` (~51 degrees, geometrically locked to the bowl shade's own opening) widened `x1.6` (clamped at ~78 degrees) to throw light more like a real pendant scattering off the shade's underside than a tight spot; `spotPenumbra` tightened `0.5 -> 0.35` so less of the cone is spent fading out; and an explicit `distance: 18.0` was added so reach no longer depends on which of `LumenGrid`'s shadow-pool slots the fixture happens to win. The bowl's own material brightened (`0x1c4a34 -> 0x3c8362`), got glossier (`roughness: 0.3 -> 0.16`, `metalness: 0.12 -> 0.35`), and picked up a low warm `emissive` so it has visible presence even at near-zero ambient, read as bounce light from the bulb it houses.
- **[WORLD] Archive's Void Ceiling Pushed Much Higher (`ChunkManager.js`):** `canopyY` for Archive raised `9.0 -> 32.0`. Physically-correct light falloff means nothing gets lit anywhere near that height regardless, so the walls now read as fading into genuine darkness above rather than terminating at a nearby, perceptible black plane.

## [v1.5.3] - 2026-08-27

_The Loop Actually Loops_

### Fixed

- **[ENGINE] The Game Was Only Winnable Once Per Browser Session (`Environment.js`):** `level0engine` has no single ending - it's a floor-descent loop, where a correct elevator "inquest" verdict advances `player.depth` and regenerates the whole world for the next floor. Three pieces of per-floor generation state lived as ad-hoc properties directly on the persistent `Environment` singleton instead of being reset alongside `macroZones`/`discoveredSectors`/etc. in `generate()`: `lockedRoomSpawned` (gates whether the Annex sector's one locked room - the *only* place the exit key can spawn - gets built at all), `dynamicExitHash` (the guaranteed "next new macro chunk becomes the EXIT sector" mechanism, also read directly by the compass to point at it), and `_annexChunkGrids` (a maze-layout cache keyed by chunk hash). All three got set once on floor 1 and never touched again. Consequence: any player who legitimately beat floor 1 found floor 2 onward permanently unwinnable - no exit key could ever spawn again, the compass actively misdirected toward floor 1's stale chunk coordinates instead of scanning for the real one, and the HUD kept saying "FIND EXIT KEY IN ANNEX" forever with no indication anything was wrong. Found via a fresh audit of the full win-condition critical path rather than a bug report - this had apparently never been noticed because reaching floor 2 at all requires a full, successful playthrough of floor 1. Now reset alongside the rest of the per-floor state in `generate()`.
