The Three Standard Questions

Every sector, blueprint, hazard, and tool in this codex is broken down into three concrete realities: What It Does (sensory presentation, gameplay effects, and player interactions), How It Works (algorithmic generation, spatial registration, and mathematical pipelines), and Why It Works (psychological design rationale, performance bounds, and zero-allocation constraints).

Volume Status: Complete

All 63 procedural subsystems are fully catalogued and source-verified against the engine repository. Reference tables and entries reflect exact constants, probability distributions, and architectural boundaries.

Normal : The Mono-Yellow Corridors

Macro Sector Baseline Hub
What It Does

The foundational starting environment of Level 0. Damp wallpaper, musty carpet, buzzing 60Hz fluorescent tubes, and sprawling standard halls that connect the complex.

How It Works

Acts as the default cell archetype in SectorPlacement.js. Synthesizes procedural carpet and wallpaper textures via ProceduralTextureFactory.js. Integrates StandardHall, AlcoveCorner, and Duct blueprints.

Why It Works

Provides the iconic liminal reference point. Its visual monotony lulls the player into disorientation, making sudden transitions into hazardous sectors jarring and terrifying.

Archive : The Paper Labyrinth

Macro Sector Dust Haze Entity: Archivist
What It Does

Dense stacks of metal document shelves rising to the ceiling, sorting tables, and scattered case files under dim, sepia-toned dust fog.

How It Works

Instantiates ArchiveSector.js, injecting high-density document spawn hooks into NarrativeProps.js. Muffles high frequencies via AcousticEngine to mimic paper sound absorption.

Why It Works

The primary research hub. Contains crucial cold-case documents, but tight sightlines and rustling paper make evading the sound-sensitive Archivist treacherous.

Atrium : Vertical Courtyards

Macro Sector Multi-Tier Verticality Echo Acoustics
What It Does

Expansive three-story courtyards with skylight grids, concrete balconies, and central elevator shafts that puncture the endless low-ceiling claustrophobia.

How It Works

AtriumSector.js clears ceiling cells across a 4×4 chunk footprint, placing modular perimeter balconies and open railings. AcousticEngine maximizes reverb time to 3.8 seconds.

Why It Works

Offers sudden scale shock. Wide visibility gives false safety, but footstep echoes carry across great distances, alerting distant predators across multiple floors.

Exit : The Subterranean Bunker

Macro Sector Victory Chamber Inquest Terminal
What It Does

Heavy blast bunker containing the Inquest Terminal, staff ephemera, and the locked industrial hoist elevator to the surface.

How It Works

ExitSector.js generates a sealed bunker sealed behind a reinforced blast airlock. Activates only when all surge breakers are thrown, orienting the Threshold Compass directly to its coordinates.

Why It Works

Acts as the climatic culmination of the gameplay loop, requiring physical maze traversal, evidence collation, and PIN deciphering.

Annex : Sterile Corporate Offices

Macro Sector Pure White Fog (#ffffff) Zero Anomaly
What It Does

A blindingly sterile corporate wing featuring modular office quadrants, desks, filing cabinets, water coolers, and fluorescent lighting under whisper-thin white fog.

How It Works

Instantiates AnnexSector.js. Configures fog: 0.005, fogColor: 0xffffff, ambient: 0.38, and lightIntensity: 2.3. Generates quadrant floorplans with partitioned desk clusters (quadrantPools) and low-reverb acoustic profiles (rt60: 0.35s, wet: 0.08).

Why It Works

Psychological subversion: shifting from dingy mono-yellow corridors into clinical, spotless white spaces induces uncanny corporate dread while providing a temporary haven from roaming entities.

Acme : The Endless Vertical Scaffolding

Macro Sector Multi-Level (40m) Lightning & Rain
What It Does

A dizzying 40-meter-tall abyss of suspended industrial catwalks, rusted ladders, hanging caged incandescent bulbs, and driving rain illuminated by sporadic distant lightning strikes.

How It Works

Generated via AcmeSector.js. Leverages multiLevelMaze: 40, voidCeiling: {y: 100000}, voidFloorY: -100000, and bottomlessRescue: true. Why It Works

Replaces horizontal claustrophobia with acute acrophobia. Vertically stacked navigation punishes missteps with dizzying falls caught by bottomless rescue safety nets while echoing footsteps across wide open gulfs.

What It Does

The primary horror force of Level 0. Roams the facility hunting by sound. Freezes while lit by the flashlight at close range, but builds rage the longer it is observed, which makes it faster once the beam drops.

How It Works
  1. Sensory Model: Calculates player noise levels against hearing distance. Exhaustion and sprinting amplify detection ranges.
  2. Cone Check: Compares the camera's forward vector against the direction to the entity. With the flashlight on, clear line of sight, distance under 25 m, and a dot product above 0.85, velocity is set to zero.
  3. Aberration FX: Drives dynamic post-processing shaders (scanlines, vignette, chromatic aberration) proportional to proximity.
Why It Works

Creates heart-pounding staredowns where looking directly at death keeps you alive, until your battery begins to die.
