Everything You Ever Wanted to Know About Level 0 Engine

The Definitive Architectural Codex, System Internals, Survival Field Manual, and Contributor Handbook

Engine v1.5.8.9 Vanilla ES6 Modules Three.js r160 (Vendored) Seeded Procedural Horror Zero Bundlers / Zero npm
The Three Inviolable Architectural Questions

In fidelity to the Level 0 Engineering Codex, every system, tool, and entity in this manual is evaluated through three lenses: What It Does (player behavior, visible manifestations, sensory feedback), How It Works (internal algorithms, web worker pipelines, DSP circuits, spatial indices), and Why It Works (psychological horror design, mathematical guarantees, and zero-allocation performance rules).

Book I: Systems Philosophy & Rules of Reality

1. The Soul of the Backrooms

In liminal fiction, the infinite yellow corridors of Level 0 are often mistaken for mere repetitive chaos. Level 0 Engine operates on a fundamentally different paradigm: The facility has memory, bureaucratic decay, and mathematical logic.

You are not wandering an abstract void; you are navigating a decommissioned corporate subterranean complex trapped in self-replicating spatial recursion. Fluorescent ballasts buzz at precise acoustic frequencies. Shift logs, coffee fund rosters, and disciplinary notices cling to desks and lockers. Behind every door lies not randomness, but a seeded deterministic world where truth can be excavated through careful fact-finding.

The Core Axioms of Level 0
  1. No Bundlers, No Build Step for Development: The repository serves directly to the browser via ES6 modules. What you edit is immediately what executes.
  2. Determinism Over Randomness: Every corridor, prop, document, and entity spawn is derived mathematically from a 32-bit seed. A single seed produces the exact same labyrinth across machines.
  3. Hot Paths Never Allocate: The player tick, collision detection, and entity perception loops operate with preallocated scratch vectors. Zero garbage collection stuttering during moments of horror.
  4. The Player is an Investigator, Not an Action Hero: Survival is attained through patience, kinetic battery management, stealth, and corroborating fragmented documentary claims.
  5. Verified Means Observed: Claims are invalid until proven by automated headless browser smoke tests or in-engine diagnostic instrumentation.

2. The Zero-Bundler Imperative

Modern web development frequently buries simple 3D experiences beneath brittle stacks of Webpack, Vite, Babel, and node_modules dependencies. Level 0 Engine rejects this ceremony.

All engine code runs as pure, un-transpiled ES6 modules loaded natively by modern browser engines. A lightweight Node HTTP server (engine_server.js) serves static files and injects cross-origin isolation headers. THREE is provided as a trusted global via locally vendored r160.js (Decision D-001). There is zero compilation time, zero bundler configuration drift, and zero risk of supply-chain package compromise.

3. Determinism as Law

In Level 0 Engine, pseudo-randomness is divided into two strict categories:

  • Structural RNG (Seeded): Seeded by a 32-bit MurmurHash integer derived from the level string. This RNG generates the Julia-set wall topology, chooses macro sector bounds, selects structural blueprints, and deals narrative case-files from data/*.json. Replaying the same seed guarantees identical layouts.
  • Cosmetic Noise (Math.random()): Reserved exclusively for ephemeral, non-reproducible visual and auditory effects: fluorescent bulb flicker jitter, film grain noise on canvas textures, and entity spawn micro-jitter.

4. The Cold Case & Fact-Finding

Unlike generic survival games with arbitrary keys, Level 0 centers on the Finding of Fact. Each seed generates a cold case surrounding a missing staff member. Documents scattered throughout the facility make specific documentary claims regarding rules, dates, and names.

Uncorroborated documents induce Paranoia, which physically degrades your kinetic flashlight's battery ceiling. To escape, the player must corroborate claims across disparate macro sectors, assemble the 4-digit PIN for the blast elevator, and submit the cold-case truth at the final Inquest Terminal.

5. Julia Maze & Chunk Streaming Pipeline

Files: src/world/ChunkManager.js, ChunkStreamer.js, ChunkBuilder.js, ChunkWorker.js

What It Does

Dynamically streams an infinite 3D labyrinth around the player in fixed-size chunks (typically 16×16 cells), building and tearing down geometries in real time without dropping frames.

How It Works
  1. Offloaded Math: ChunkStreamer tracks the player's grid coordinates and calculates chunks within streaming radius. Requests are dispatched to ChunkWorker.js running on a separate Web Worker thread.
  2. Julia Set Formulation: The worker computes wall topologies by iterating a 2D complex rational map. Cells where \(|z| > \text{threshold}\) collapse into walls, while interior coordinates carve out open hallways.
  3. Geometry Synthesis: ChunkBuilder receives cell matrices from the worker, applies macro sector rules from Sectors.js, selects structural blueprints from StructuralBlueprints.js, and stitches meshes via StructureKit.js.
  4. Batch Disposal: As chunks exit the streaming envelope, buffers, collision lines, and dynamic light instances are cleanly disposed of to reclaim GPU VRAM.
Why It Works

Offloading fractal computation to a web worker guarantees that the main JavaScript execution thread never hitches. The render loop maintains locked 60fps even as massive macro sectors materialize on the horizon.

6. LumenGrid & Shadow Hysteresis

Files: src/aesthetics/LumenGrid.js, src/aesthetics/RectAreaLightUniforms.js

What It Does

Manages the atmospheric illumination of Level 0, powering fluorescent ceiling panels, emergency strobe conduits, breaker blackout cascades, and cast shadows.

How It Works
  1. Ceiling Area Lights: Utilizes vendored RectAreaLightUniformsLib to simulate rectangular diffuse fixtures without the sharp artificial artifacts of point lights.
  2. Fixed Shadow Caster Pool: Real-time shadows on mobile and integrated GPUs are expensive. LumenGrid maintains a strictly capped budget (e.g., 4 active shadow casters) allocated by proximity to the player camera.
  3. Distance Hysteresis: Lights crossing the shadow-caster threshold utilize a 2-unit hysteresis buffer, preventing visible shadow popping when the player oscillates near a boundary.
Why It Works

Enforces hardware performance predictability. The engine achieves rich, claustrophobic volumetric atmosphere while respecting mobile WebGL texture unit and shadow map limits.

7. Web Audio DSP & Real-Time Occlusion

Files: src/audio/AcousticEngine.js, Mixer.js, Foley.js

What It Does

The auditory heart of Level 0. Synthesizes procedural room tones, footstep impacts, fluorescent hums, heartbeat adrenaline pumps, and physically muffles sound when blocked by walls.

How It Works
  1. Procedural Synthesis: Tone generators create layered sine, sawtooth, and filtered noise sources for the fluorescent hum (60Hz ground hum with micro-phase harmonics).
  2. Raycast Occlusion Filter: When spatial audio sources (e.g. entity groans or radio static) emit, AcousticEngine casts rays through the SpatialHashGrid to the player. Each intersecting wall applies an exponential low-pass BiquadFilter, creating realistic wall dampening.
  3. Exhaustion DSP: When the player sprint stamina or oxygen runs dry, a master dynamics compressor and low-pass filter squashes world audio, elevating internal bodily respiration and erratic heartbeats.
Why It Works

Horror relies more heavily on acoustics than visuals. Hearing an entity's muffled gait around two corners prompts genuine dread, informing player tactical decisions before visual contact occurs.

8. Procedural Canvas Textures & Material Library

Files: src/aesthetics/ProceduralTextureFactory.js, MaterialLibrary.js

What It Does

Generates all wall, floor, ceiling, and metal textures dynamically at runtime via HTML5 Canvas 2D drawing routines, with zero network asset downloads.

How It Works
  1. Canvas Synthesis: ProceduralTextureFactory draws perlin noise, stained watermarks, worn wallpaper seams, subway tile grout, and acoustic tile perforations onto off-screen canvas elements.
  2. Mipmap Generation: Canvases are converted to THREE.CanvasTexture instances with anisotropic filtering enabled, and cached in MaterialLibrary.js.
  3. Static Fallback: If WebGL context creation constraints or dev tuners require it, textures can fall back to pre-baked PNGs in assets/textures/.
Why It Works

Shrinks initial page load to under 2 megabytes while eliminating HTTP request roundtrips. Every surface carries procedural variation, avoiding obvious tiling repetition.

9. Dual-Key LocalStorage Persistence

Files: src/system/SaveManager.js

What It Does

Maintains player session progress, active cold-case leads, gathered document inventory, pulled surge breakers, and custom engine settings.

How It Works
  1. Dual-Key Safeguard: Before writing new game state to localStorage['level0_state'], SaveManager copies the existing state to level0_state_backup.
  2. Integrity Check: On engine boot, JSON parsing is wrapped in strict try/catch blocks. If corruption is detected, the backup is automatically restored, and telemetry logs a warning.
  3. Purge Mechanics: "Purge Memory" resets the session while preserving player settings; "Purge & Start New Game" restores baseline seed state.
Why It Works

Protects player progress against accidental browser closures, battery failures, or localStorage write crashes.

Book III: Procedural Topography & Macro-Sectors

The maze hierarchy operates in three distinct structural tiers:

  • Macro Sectors: Large themed geographic zones (Archive, Atrium, Annex, etc.) spanning many contiguous chunks, defined by distinct wall palettes, fog colors, audio hums, and lighting profiles in Sectors.js.
  • Chunks: The 16×16 streaming unit built by ChunkWorker.js and assembled by ChunkBuilder.js.
  • Structural Blueprints: Architectural micro-features (Ducts, Arches, Crevices, Faraday Cages) placed within open cell spaces, registered with strict probability weights in StructuralBlueprints.js.

Book IV: Survival Field Manual (How to Play)

12. The Kinetic Flashlight & Crank Physics

Files: src/player/Flashlight.js, src/player/SomaticInput.js

What It Does

Your primary survival instrument and psychological shield. The heavy flashlight illuminates dark corridors and freezes the Anomaly in its beam, but rapidly drains power.

How It Works
  1. Kinetic Charging: The flashlight has no disposable batteries. It is charged mechanically by sprinting (converting movement inertia) or by rapidly shaking the camera (cranking the dyno mechanism).
  2. Parabolic Beam: Casts a focused central beam with quadratic distance attenuation and a wider subtle ambient cone.
  3. Flicker Warning: When charge drops below 15%, the bulb begins irregular brownout flickers, reducing beam efficacy and triggering entity agitation.
Why It Works

Forces dynamic physical engagement. Recharging demands noisy, erratic actions (shaking or running) that risk attracting nearby entities, creating high-tension tactical dilemmas.

13. Paranoia & The Corroboration Loop

Files: src/narrative/StoryEngine.js, src/player/PlayerController.js

What It Does

A direct mechanical bridge connecting narrative discovery with player physical survival. Reading uncorroborated documents increases Paranoia, which places a hard cap on your maximum flashlight battery charge.

How It Works
  1. Unverified Claims: When you read a document making an uncorroborated claim (e.g. "Rule 14 was enacted in 1984"), paranoia ticks up, capping battery maximum to 80%, 60%, or lower.
  2. Cross-Sector Corroboration: To bleed off paranoia and restore full battery capacity, you must explore and locate a second document in a different macro sector that corroborates the identical claim.
  3. Resolution: Corroborated claims turn green in your PDA Journal (J), permanently dispelling paranoia and solidifying an objective truth for the Inquest.
Why It Works

Prevents lore from being treated as passive background decoration. Reading and understanding the narrative is a mandatory survival requirement.

14. Somatic Movement, Exhaustion & Compression

Files: src/player/PlayerController.js, src/system/SomaticController.js

What It Does

Models realistic physical presence: inertia, footstep cadence, wall impacts, and squeezing through tight bottlenecks.

How It Works
  1. Wall Collisions & Camera Trauma: Crashing into walls at high speed jolts the camera with physics trauma, triggers a heavy dull thud, and rattles your flashlight.
  2. Terminal Exhaustion: Sprinting burns oxygen twice as fast. When exhausted, the player stumbles, hearing muffled breathing DSP, while their hearing radius expands—making them easier for the Anomaly to detect.
  3. Compression Mode (Q): Pressing Q lowers your camera and narrows collision extents, allowing you to compress through tight ductwork, collapsed crevices, and floor vents.
Why It Works

Grounds the player in a fragile physical body. You cannot simply sprint blindly through corridors without suffering disorientation and acoustic penalties.

15. Navigation Tools: Compass, Paintball & PDA

Files: src/player/Compass.js, src/player/PaintballGun.js, src/ui/JournalUI.js

What It Does

A specialized triad of non-lethal navigation instruments designed to combat spatial disorientation in infinite geometry.

How It Works
  1. The Threshold Compass (M): A magnetized brass instrument. Its needle points toward the nearest unvisited macro-sector blast door. Once all surge breakers are pulled, it redirects toward the newly spawned Exit Bunker.
  2. Paintball Gun & UV Paint (P): Fires luminescent procedural paint splatters against walls and floors. Marks glow in the dark, allowing you to leave breadcrumb trails at confusing intersections.
  3. The PDA Journal (J): Opens an in-game personal digital assistant with a virtual cursor to review discovered notes, active leads, and corroborated case facts.
Why It Works

Provides essential orienteering capabilities without breaking immersion with artificial mini-maps or immersion-shattering HUD overlays.

16. The Assembled Lock & Inquest Escape

Files: src/narrative/ExitTerminal.js, src/world/sectors/ExitSector.js

What It Does

The ultimate victory condition of Level 0 Engine. Unlock the blast elevator, submit the Finding of Fact, and ascend to the surface.

How It Works
  1. The 4-Digit Assembled Lock: The exit elevator door is sealed by a mechanical PIN assembled from three distinct clues scattered across the complex: the Rule Number, the Enactment Year, and the Pen Color Code.
  2. The Finding of Fact: At the glowing green terminal inside the Exit Bunker, you are prompted to present the true sequence of events regarding the cold case.

Transforms escape into an intellectual triumph. Victory requires both physical mastery of the maze and mental mastery of its lore.

Book V: Hazards, Entities & Phenomena

17. The Anomaly (The Roaming Predator)

Files: src/entities/Anomaly.js

What It Does

The default macro predator of Level 0. A fast, relentless entity that roams the halls hunting the player by acoustic signature and line-of-sight.

How It Works
  1. Acoustic Hearing Radius: Calculates player noise generation (sneaking: 2m, walking: 8m, sprinting: 20m, terminal exhaustion stumble: 35m).
  2. The 30-Degree Light Cone Freeze: If the player turns to face the Anomaly and catches it directly within the central 30-degree cone of their kinetic flashlight, the Anomaly freezes in place, screeching with rage.
  3. Rage Buildup: Freezing is temporary. Each second the beam holds it, the Anomaly accumulates rage. When your battery flickers out or you look away, it surges forward with increased velocity!
  4. Proximity Aberration: As it approaches, the screen suffers severe chromatic aberration, audio crushing, and claustrophobic vignettes.
Why It Works

Inverts conventional horror tropes: facing the monster saves you, but locks you in a terrifying staredown while your flashlight battery drains towards zero.

19. Surge Breakers & Blackout Cascades

Heavy high-voltage electrical levers found on walls. Pulling them with E disrupts local facility power, opening secured airlocks while plunging sectors into pitch blackness.

How It Works
  1. Blackout Cascade: Pulling the lever instantly shatters local fluorescent tubes with particle debris and sound FX.
  2. Cascade Delay: Neighboring lights sequentially extinguish down the hall in a terrifying domino wave.
  3. Emergency Reboot: After a timed blackout duration, emergency amber or red warning strobes initiate an irregular flicking reboot sequence.
Why It Works

Delivers visceral, player-initiated environmental shock. You must willingly plunge yourself into total darkness to progress through locked gates.

21. Seeded Narrative Dealing

Files: src/narrative/StoryEngine.js, data/*.json

What It Does

Generates a coherent, non-repeating cold-case mystery from thousands of narrative fragments stored in JSON data files.

How It Works
  1. Card Dealing: On level generation, StoryEngine creates a shuffled deck of clues, foreshadowing snippets, and lore logs seeded by the level seed.
  2. Data Repositories: Draws from clues.json, lore.json, foreshadow.json, finales.json, and ephemera.json.
  3. Placement: Distributes documents onto generated desks, file cabinets, and clipboards as chunks materialize around the player.
Why It Works

Ensures that no two runs tell the exact same story, while guaranteeing that essential clues required to assemble the PIN code are always placed in reachable sectors.

23. JSEP Logic & Safe AST Templating

Files: src/utils/SafeEval.js, lore-editor/js/rendering.js

What It Does

Safely evaluates complex display conditions (e.g. ctx.sector === 'ANNEX' && c.lead) and templates variables into text without dangerous JavaScript eval() or new Function().

How It Works
  1. Vendored JSEP: Expression strings are parsed into an Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) using vendored jsep.min.js.
  2. AST Walk: SafeEval.js evaluates identifiers and operators against a sanitized context object.
  3. XSS Sanitization: Before rendered text touches the DOM, it is passed through vendored purify.min.js (DOMPurify).
Why It Works

Enforces complete security isolation (Decision D-001). Community lore expansions or user-edited JSON files cannot execute arbitrary JavaScript in the player's browser.

25. AST Pre-Save Validation

Files: lore-editor/js/rendering.js

What It Does

Intercepts Lore Editor saves and verifies that all conditions, variables, threads, and sector references exist, preventing broken entries from entering the game.

How It Works
  1. Pre-Save Hook: When the author clicks "Save", runPreSaveConditionChecks parses all condition expressions with JSEP.
  2. Cross-File Audit: Verifies that every referenced variable exists in variables.json, every thread in threads.json, and every sector in Sectors.js.
  3. Save Abort: If an unknown identifier is detected, saving is aborted with a clear diagnostic dialog.
Why It Works

Guarantees data integrity at the authoring boundary, catching typos before they can manifest as runtime errors during gameplay.

26. In-Engine Atmosphere & Light Tuners

Files: src/ui/AtmosphereTuner.js, LightTuner.js, SectorTunerFactory.js

What It Does

Live, in-game developer sliders that allow real-time tuning of fog density, light intensity, and colors, with instant disk persistence.

How It Works
  1. Interactive UI: Pressing dev keys displays interactive sliders over the 3D viewport.

Why It Works

Eliminates the tedious loop of tweaking numbers in code, reloading, and checking. Lighting designers can sculpt the horror atmosphere directly inside the running scene.

Book VIII: The Proving Ground

"Verified Means Observed"

In Level 0 Engine, code that looks correct in a code review is not considered complete until it has been proven by automated observation in a real browser engine.

27. Headless Playwright Smoke Tests

Files: test/p4_01_smoke.test.js

What It Does

An automated headless Chromium integration suite that launches the game, traverses all 14 macro sectors, and verifies procedural generation is completely error-free.

How It Works
  1. Headless Boot: Spawns Chromium via Playwright, loads http://localhost:8080/engine.html, and monitors console errors.
  2. Targeted Sector Warp: Extracts explicit chunk coordinates from SectorPlacement.js for fixed seed 0x12345678. Warps camera to each sector and forces ChunkStreamer generation.
  3. Assertion Matrix: Verifies that all meshes, materials, and lights compile without WebGL warnings or uncaught exceptions.
Why It Works

Guarantees that generator changes or sector refactors cannot silently introduce broken imports or geometry synthesis crashes.

28. Frame-Time Baseline Budgets

Files: test/perf_baseline.json

What It Does

Enforces an automated CPU generation budget across all sectors to prevent performance regressions from creeping into the engine.

How It Works
  1. Timing Capture: During headless smoke runs, the test measures total milliseconds spent in environment.genStats.totalMs.
  2. Baseline Comparison: Compares results against stored measurements in test/perf_baseline.json.
  3. Regression Gate: If CPU generation time regresses by more than 1.5x against the baseline, the test suite fails automatically.
Why It Works

Keeps procedural generation lean and fast, ensuring low-end laptops and mobile browsers maintain silky 60fps performance.

Book IX: Contributor's Protocol

29. Engineering Conventions & Invariants

What It Does

Strict software engineering rules governing all code contributions to the repository.

How It Works
  • No Per-Frame Allocations: Hot paths (player movement, entity pathfinding, spatial hash queries) must reuse preallocated scratch vectors. Never call new THREE.Vector3() inside render loops!
  • Pure ES6 Modules: Never add bundlers, transpilers, or Babel configurations.
  • THREE is a Global: Never write import * as THREE from 'three'. The global is supplied by r160.js.
  • Sum to 1.0000: Probabilities registered in StructuralBlueprints.js must sum to exactly 1.0000.
Why It Works

Preserves maximum performance, eliminates GC stuttering, and keeps the engine universally runnable in any browser without build tooling.
